Expressive Navigation

Real-time affect-aware clinical decision support on iOS

Role
iOS Engineer & Researcher
Context
MIT Media Lab, Fluid Interfaces Group
Tech Stack
Swift, UIKit, Apple Vision Framework

Overview

Expressive Navigation is an affect-aware iOS prototype that uses Apple's Vision framework to infer subtle facial-expression cues in real time and surfaces actionable, clinician-facing feedback to support therapeutic interactions. Everything runs entirely on-device — no video, images, or audio ever leave the phone.

How might we give clinicians a real-time, privacy-preserving signal about a patient's emotional state — one that supports better therapeutic rapport without replacing clinical judgment?
Swift UIKit Apple Vision AVFoundation Computer Vision Affect Computing On-Device ML

Problem & Motivation

Clinicians navigate complex emotional terrain in every therapeutic session. Subtle shifts in a patient's expression — a slight brow furrow, a tightening around the mouth, a drop in eye contact — are critical cues that inform therapeutic decisions: when to probe deeper, when to slow down, when to change approach.

But these signals are fleeting, easy to miss when managing a conversation, and impossible to review afterward. Meanwhile, facial expression analysis research typically requires expensive lab equipment, privacy-invasive video recording, and offline processing — none of which works in a live clinical setting.

Expressive Navigation asks: what if the clinician's phone could act as a real-time, privacy-preserving emotional compass?

Core Capabilities

👁️
Real-Time Landmark Detection

Streams the front camera and runs Apple Vision's face landmark detection on every frame — extracting eyes, brows, nose, mouth, and head pose in real time.

📊
Affect Inference

Converts facial geometry into interpretable expression features (smile, brow raise, brow furrow, eye openness, gaze) mapped onto valence × arousal coordinates.

🎯
Per-Person Calibration

A short calibration step records the individual's resting face so all features are measured as deviations from their unique neutral baseline.

💬
Clinical Feedback Engine

Debounced, rule-based guidance surfaced to the clinician with severity level, rationale, and actionable suggestion — never interrupting flow.

📈
Signal Dynamics

EMA smoothing with trend detection (warming/cooling), variability tracking (lability vs. blunting), and time-in-state metrics across the session.

🔒
Privacy-First Recording

Session export captures only derived numeric signals (valence, arousal, state) as on-device JSON — zero imagery or audio ever leaves the device.

How the Inference Works

Apple Vision returns landmark points and head pose only. The AffectEngine derives expression features using scale-invariant geometry — everything normalized by interocular distance for robustness across different face sizes and distances:

  • Smile — vertical position of mouth corners within the mouth's span, combined with mouth widening
  • Brow raise — eyebrow-to-eye distance vs. calibrated baseline
  • Brow furrow — inner-brow separation (corrugator proxy) vs. baseline
  • Eye openness — eye-aspect-ratio (EAR)
  • Mouth opening — inner-lip vertical gap
  • Gaze/attention — head yaw/pitch magnitude as a proxy for eye contact

These features map onto valence (pleasantness) and arousal (activation) via a weighted, fully-inspectable model, then into one of eight discrete expression states on the affect circumplex:

Positive Engaged Calm Surprised Tense Distressed Withdrawn Fatigued

Clinical framing: This is decision-support only — not a diagnostic device and not a measurement of true internal emotion. Facial expression is an imperfect, culturally-mediated signal. The output is treated as one weak signal among many, always with a qualified human in the loop.

Privacy & Ethics

Affect computing with facial data in a clinical context demands the highest standard of privacy and ethical framing:

📵
Zero Imagery Stored

No video, images, or audio are ever saved or transmitted. Only derived numeric signals exist after processing.

📱
Fully On-Device

All inference runs locally using Apple Vision. No network calls, no cloud processing, no third-party SDKs.

⚖️
Decision-Support Only

Explicitly framed as one weak signal among many. Never diagnostic, always requiring a qualified human in the loop.

Key Implementation Details

Scale Invariance
Interocular Normalization

All facial measurements are normalized by interocular distance (computed in aspect-correct pixel space), making the system robust to different face sizes, camera distances, and head angles.

Calibration
Per-Person Baseline

A 5-sample calibration captures the individual's resting neutral face. All subsequent measurements are deviations from their personal baseline — not from a generic "average face."

Temporal
EMA + Debouncing

Exponential moving average smoothing prevents frame-to-frame jitter. The feedback engine uses per-rule cooldowns (14s) to avoid alert fatigue — only sustained patterns trigger guidance.

Quality Gating
Signal Confidence

The system gates all clinical guidance on signal quality — poor face detection, extreme angles, or low landmark confidence suppress feedback rather than generating noisy false signals.

Outcome & Reflection

Expressive Navigation demonstrates that meaningful affect inference is possible using only Apple's built-in Vision framework — no custom ML models, no cloud APIs, no privacy compromise — while maintaining honest epistemic framing about what facial expressions can and cannot tell us.

The project pushed my understanding of real-time computer vision pipelines, signal processing (EMA smoothing, trend detection), and the deeply important question of how to surface probabilistic information to experts without over-claiming. The hardest design problem wasn't technical — it was finding the right epistemic humility in how the system communicates uncertainty.

Future Directions

  • Longitudinal session comparison — tracking affect trajectory across multiple appointments
  • Explore ARKit's blendshape coefficients (52 facial action units) for richer input when available
  • Clinician user-testing to validate feedback utility and refine debounce/cooldown parameters
  • Configurable sensitivity profiles for different therapeutic modalities (CBT vs. psychodynamic)
