Iteration 11 · what came out of it

Six variations refined the meter itself. The version carrying a percentage label was chosen: the bar communicates the shape of the gap while the number pins it down. Dual numbers were rejected for making the reader perform the division herself, and the compact inline treatment for shrinking the bar until the gap stopped being legible.
