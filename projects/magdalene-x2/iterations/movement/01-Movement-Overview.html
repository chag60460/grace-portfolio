Iteration 1 · what came out of it

Movement's job is to answer a single question each morning — how hard should I train today — read through the cycle. Three concepts were built out in full rather than argued on paper: a readiness ring, familiar and glanceable but at risk of feeling like just another wearable; a cycle phase-arc, the most differentiated view but the one needing the most teaching; and a guidance-first screen, the calmest and most on-voice. The choice is a judgment about feel, which only real screens can settle.
