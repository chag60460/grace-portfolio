Iteration 3 · what came out of it

A new Activities section was tried in three layouts. The grid of tiles was adopted and carried directly into the next iteration. List rows were rejected as the densest but flattest option, collapsing every sport into an identical row, and sparkline cards for showing too few activities at once; the grid keeps all six types visible while giving each a recognisable identity.
