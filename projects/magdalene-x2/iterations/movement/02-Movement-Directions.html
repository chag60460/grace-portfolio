Iteration 2 · what came out of it

The same content was given three different leads and compared side by side. The guidance-led reading was adopted as the default because it matches the product principle of saying what something means rather than how it works, while the fitness-rings dashboard was rejected for leading with numbers the user must interpret herself. The phase-arc remains swappable, being the one view no competitor offers.
