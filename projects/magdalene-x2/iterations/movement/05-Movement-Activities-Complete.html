Iteration 5 · what came out of it

The Activities section was assembled into a single live screen: a this-week summary, the grid, per-activity detail on tap, and the full logging flow. Keeping logging in a separate section was rejected, since reviewing the week and adding to it happen in the same sitting, and splitting them would mean navigating away in the middle of the task.
