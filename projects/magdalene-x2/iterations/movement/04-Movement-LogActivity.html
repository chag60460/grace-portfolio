Movement — log an activity

Building forward from the Grid Activities screen: how you log a workout. Tap an activity tile (or + Log an activity) → pick the type → enter the details → a saved confirmation that ties it to your cycle. The entry form is the key screen, so it's shown two ways — a structured form vs a quick visual one. Both phones are live: tap a tile or + Log, fill in, Save, ‹ back.

Iteration 4 · what came out of it

Building forward from the grid, this round defined how a workout gets logged: tap a tile or + Log an activity, choose the type, enter the details, and receive a confirmation tied to the cycle. Because logging is the action repeated most often, the entry form was deliberately shown two ways — structured fields, which are familiar and precise, against large steppers and effort chips, which are faster and more tactile.
