Iteration 11 · what came out of it

A single Manage data entry was placed beside the sync status, opening either Import, which pulls history from Flo, Clue or Apple Health, or manual entry of dates, flow and symptoms. Hand-backfilling was rejected as the primary path: cycle history is the one dataset a new user already holds somewhere else, and asking her to retype months of it would lose her before the product could say anything useful. This entry pattern went on to become the convention for every other module.
