Cycle — circle / ring concepts

Scheme locked to your picks: Blush Peach overall + Coral-Pastel calendar. The only thing changing across these six is the circle — softer, gentler treatments instead of the hard 4-color pie. Which circle feels right?

1 · Soft Gradient
Phases blend smoothly — no hard edges. The gentle version of the current ring.
2 · Day Dots
One dot per day, colored by phase, today enlarged. Clean & modern (Clue/Oura-ish).
3 · Minimal Progress
A single soft arc showing how far through your cycle you are. The calmest.
4 · Soft Segments
Four gentle arcs separated by small gaps — clearer phases, less pie-chart.
5 · Day Ticks
Delicate ticks around the ring; period & fertile days marked. Light & precise.
6 · Single-Hue Bloom
One warm gradient ring, phase named in the center. Softest & most minimal.

Iteration 7 · what came out of it

With the palette fixed, the only variable across these six was the circle itself. Day Dots was chosen: one dot per day, coloured by phase, with today enlarged. The gentler alternatives each bought calm at the cost of precision — Minimal Progress shows how far through the cycle you are but not which phase, and Single-Hue Bloom names the phase but loses the shape of the cycle. Day Dots reads softly while still accounting for every individual day.
