Iteration 5 · what came out of it

Six pastels drawn from Flo's own palette — lavender and periwinkle backgrounds, a signature coral-pink accent, and blush tints. Coral Pastel was kept and later locked in as the calendar palette. The lavender and periwinkle bases, including Flo Classic and Plum Rose, were rejected for reading as a direct impression of the competitor rather than an identity of Magdalene's own.
