Iteration 2 · what came out of it

Variant D was adopted: Variant C's glanceable ring stacked above Variant B's month calendar. The two questions a cycle screen has to answer — where am I in my cycle, and on which dates — proved equally non-negotiable, so neither single-element option survived on its own. Variant A's phase sentence was cut in the process; once the ring states the phase, repeating it in prose underneath adds nothing but noise.
