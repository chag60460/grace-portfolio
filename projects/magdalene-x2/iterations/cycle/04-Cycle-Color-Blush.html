Iteration 4 · what came out of it

Six variations within the warm-pink family, ranging from dusty through coral to rosé gold. No single option was locked: Dusty Rose read muted and sophisticated, Rose Mauve moody, and Rosé Gold luxe, each pulling the mood somewhere unintended. The family was clearly right and the exact temperature clearly was not, so the exploration widened into pastels rather than forcing a decision.
