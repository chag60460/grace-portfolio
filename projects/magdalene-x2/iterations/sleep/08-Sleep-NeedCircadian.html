Iteration 8 · what came out of it

Sleep need and circadian rhythm were built as nested pages, with a chronotype quiz and two routes to recalibration. Requiring a seven-night calibration week as the only path was rejected: it is the accurate route, but a dead end for anyone wanting an answer today. Recalculating from nights already logged runs alongside it, with the window and night filters exposed so the estimate stays honest about what it rests on.
