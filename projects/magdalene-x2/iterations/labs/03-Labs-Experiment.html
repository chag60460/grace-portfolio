Iteration 3 · what came out of it

Every marker now opens its own data-driven detail, but an experiment is offered only where there is something to act on. Attaching one to in-range markers was rejected: HbA1c at 5.2% has no intervention worth running, and offering one anyway would manufacture a problem and undercut the product's claim to provide information rather than alarm. In-range markers still route to the clinician report.
