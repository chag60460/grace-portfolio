Iteration 1 · what came out of it

This first full pass grouped markers by body system, each opening its own detail with a trend over time, normal against optimal range, cycle context, and a plain-language reading. A flat table of values was rejected outright: a lab report already is a table, and it is precisely what leaves people none the wiser. Grouping by system gives an untrained reader somewhere to start, and separating normal from optimal explains why an in-range number can still deserve attention.
