Overview

Magdalene X² is an autonomous, multi-agent health-intelligence companion that unifies a woman's labs, menstrual cycle, nutrition, fitness, and sleep into one system. It detects meaningful changes, explains them in plain language, generates hypotheses, and runs small closed-loop personal experiments — framed as wellness insight, never diagnosis.

Every woman deserves a tireless health analyst that understands her whole biology — not five disconnected apps. How might we turn the data a woman already produces into understanding and action?
Product Design Multi-Agent AI Health Tech N-of-1 Experiments Femtech UX Research

Problem & Motivation

Health-engaged women generate more personal health data than ever — rings, watches, CGMs, lab panels, cycle apps — yet no tool connects it. They get numbers, not meaning, and they're left to play detective across five domains that biologically interact.

The menstrual cycle is a master modulator — it affects glucose response, sleep architecture, injury risk, and nutrition needs. Any single-domain reading (e.g., HRV or a lab value) is biologically ambiguous without cycle context. Yet no consumer tool reads these domains together.

Design Process

I explored five visual directions by studying well-designed health and femtech apps, then converged on a direction that combines Headspace-style modularity (clear, grouped modules) with Co–Star-style personalization (a voice that knows you).

Phase 1 — Research
UI Case Study

Analyzed 12+ health and femtech apps across five visual directions (calm dark-premium, warm editorial, clinical data-forward, bold athletic, personality-driven) to identify patterns worth borrowing.

Phase 2 — Concept
Low-Fi Concept

Defined the design language — warm cream palette, editorial serif for the "voice," clean sans for data. Five core screens: Today, Modules, Insight, Ask, and Goals.

Phase 3 — Iteration
Design Iterations

Refined typography (Fraunces + Inter), interaction patterns, and the experiment flow through multiple rounds. Added interactive tab navigation and smooth transitions.

Phase 4 — Hi-Fi
Hi-Fi Prototype

Full-fidelity interactive prototype with all five screens, interactive elements, and the complete insight → experiment → outcome flow.

Key Design Patterns Borrowed

  • One number per domain first — never a table on the home screen (inspired by Oura, WHOOP)
  • Color = state, not alarm — green/amber mean "where you are," not "good/bad"
  • Plain-English "what this means" after any data point (Function, WHOOP)
  • Drill-down, not dump — full data is 2–3 taps deep, never the default
  • Deviation & rest = information, not failure — anti-guilt framing throughout

Product Design

The final design uses a warm, editorial voice paired with modular data cards. The cycle phase is a first-class variable that contextualizes every insight.

System Architecture

The product loop: Ingest → Detect → Explain → Hypothesize → Experiment → Learn. The experiment engine writing outcomes back to the timeline is what closes the loop — the core differentiator.

📊

Wearables, cycle, labs, CGM, nutrition → unified health timeline, cycle-phase tagged

🔍

🤖
Reason

→
🛡️
Guard
Safety layer screens outputs — no diagnostic claims, honest uncertainty, clinician escalation
→
💬
Surface
Plain-language insights, experiments, weekly summary, clinician report

UX Principles

💡
Say what it means, not how it works

Lead with the insight, hide the machinery. The user sees the meaning — not the multi-agent pipeline behind it.

🎯
One clear next action

Every insight offers at most one small experiment. Reduce decision fatigue to a single "yes."

⚖️
Honest uncertainty

Never over-claim. Show confidence levels, defer to clinicians, and always frame as "information, not a diagnosis."

🔒
Privacy you can feel

End-to-end encryption, no third-party data sharing, granular consent. Visible control over data as a trust signal — especially for reproductive data.

Outcome & Status

Magdalene X² targets a category-creation white space that the market is visibly self-organizing toward — but no incumbent has executed: cross-domain synthesis + autonomous agents + a closed experimentation loop.

The project produced a comprehensive product requirements document, deep competitive and scientific research, three validated user personas, a full UI case study across 12+ competitor apps, and an interactive hi-fi prototype — all the artifacts needed to move from concept to build.

Next Steps

  • MVP (Mia): Cycle-aware training/fatigue insights; guided experiments; multi-source integration; retention instrumentation.
  • GA (Priya): Handle hormonal/metabolic complexity; clinician-ready reports; scaled autonomous experiment engine.
